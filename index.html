To my baby, with all my heart

And if you ever forget how loved you are, come back to this page.

My beloved Chelly, I wish I could put this whole website into your hands, sit beside you, and let you read every word while I watch your face light up. Since I can’t do that right now, I made you this little corner of my heart instead. You are so much more than the beautiful things you create. I love the artist in you — the way you turn feelings into colors and little ideas into something real — but I love the person behind the art even more. I love your heart, your voice, your smile, your softness, your strength, and all the tiny things that make you unmistakably you. I know being far apart can be hard. There are moments when I wish the distance would just fold itself up so I could be there beside you. But until that day comes, I want to keep choosing you in the ways I can: through the messages I send, the time I make for you, the patience I practice, the songs I write, and the little reminders that you never have to earn my care by being happy or having everything figured out. You don’t have to be perfect to be loved. You don’t have to be okay every second to deserve gentleness. On your bright days, I’ll celebrate with you; on your heavy days, I’ll listen and take things one moment at a time with you. And when I can’t fix what hurts, I can still remind you that you matter, that your feelings deserve care, and that you don’t have to explain everything perfectly to be heard. Thank you for being part of my life. Thank you for the comfort you give me without even realizing it. Thank you for letting me love you, and for giving my music someone so special to sing about. If I could paint you a picture with sound, I think it would be a soft melody that keeps finding its way home. And if you could paint what you mean to me, I don’t think one canvas would be enough. So here’s my promise, mylove: I’ll keep trying, keep learning how to love you well, and keep making room for the little joys we build together. One day at a time, one message at a time, one page at a time. No matter how many miles sit between us tonight, you are loved here. You are appreciated here. You are thought of here. Happy little corner of the universe, my Chelly. ♡
